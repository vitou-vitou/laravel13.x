# 06: Grounded AI Assistant & Human Escalation

**What to build:** An automated triage chatbot assistant for natural-language inquiries grounded strictly in hospital policy guides and FAQs, featuring a 1-click seamless handoff into the live staff queue.

**Blocked by:** 05: Live Customer Service Chat & Staff Triage Desk

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Patient can ask natural-language questions in the chat window (e.g. "Can I bring my child during visiting hours?", "Do I need to fast before my ultrasound?").
- [ ] AI assistant responds using only verified hospital handbook and department guidelines.
- [ ] Every automated response carries a visible non-clinical disclaimer reminding the patient to call emergency services (e.g. 911/119) for acute medical distress.
- [ ] If the patient's inquiry cannot be answered or if the patient requests human help, the bot smoothly escalates the thread into the staff triage inbox without losing conversation history.
