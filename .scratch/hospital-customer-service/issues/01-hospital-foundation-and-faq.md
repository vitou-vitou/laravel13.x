# 01: Hospital Foundation & Guest FAQ Decision Tree

**What to build:** A public, mobile-first landing experience for patients and visitors to browse hospital departments, operating hours, and navigate a deterministic FAQ decision tree (e.g. visiting policies, test preparation instructions, specialty directory) without requiring login.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** done

- [x] Guest visitor can browse active hospital departments and their physical counter locations and hours.
- [x] Guest visitor can click through a step-by-step FAQ triage decision tree to find answers to common inquiries (e.g. "Visiting Hours", "Blood Test Preparation", "Emergency vs Outpatient").
- [x] Search input allows filtering FAQs and department services instantly.
- [x] Clear disclaimer displayed stating this portal handles customer service and non-clinical inquiries only.
- [x] Admin / staff can manage departments and FAQ entries.
