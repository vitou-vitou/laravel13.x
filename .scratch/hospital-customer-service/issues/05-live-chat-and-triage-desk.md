# 05: Live Customer Service Chat & Staff Triage Desk

**What to build:** Real-time customer service messaging between patients and hospital support agents, backed by a staff triage desk with incoming queue management, department reassignment, and ticket resolution.

**Blocked by:** 01: Hospital Foundation & Guest FAQ Decision Tree

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Patient can initiate a live inquiry conversation from mobile or desktop with subject/category selection.
- [ ] Patient and support agent exchange messages in real-time with timestamp and read indicators.
- [ ] Staff triage desk displays active conversations with unread badges, waiting duration, and assigned agent.
- [ ] Staff agent can reassign an active inquiry to a different specialized hospital department desk (e.g. from General CS to Billing/Insurance or Records).
- [ ] Staff agent can resolve and close inquiries with internal resolution notes.
