The 7pj Journey (What "Live" Means)

A product is live when all 3 levels work end-to-end:

  1. L1: Quote — create, edit, hydrate (fill fields), print PDF
  2. L2: Policy — convert quote → policy, edit, print PDF
  3. L3: Endorsement — create endorsement, edit, print PDF

9 lines × 3 levels = 27 journeys live (Marine is 1 journey despite 2 codes).

CEO Translation

"We have 9 insurance products live. Each one supports the full lifecycle: quote, policy, endorsement. 3 more products are in development and not customer-facing yet."

Risk Awareness: Legacy Products (Don't Touch)

Five legacy codes exist but are out of scope for Direct Book:

  • 0121 Public Liability
  • 0122 Fire
  • 0123 Property
  • 0124 Home Package
  • 0125 Business Package

Never refactor these. They predate Direct Book, have different architecture, and are production-stable. Any change risks breaking live customer policies.
