# Laravel usage by country

No single official "monthly active Laravel users by country" table exists. Laravel Inc doesn't publish one; laravel.com traffic stats and BuiltWith site-detection counts are the closest public proxies. Ranking below blends both, most-used first.

## Ranked list (best-available proxy, descending)

1. United States — largest raw site count (BuiltWith: 362k–856k live sites depending on snapshot; ~32% of all Laravel sites)
2. India — 2nd largest by BuiltWith count (~17% share); also #2 source of laravel.com traffic (5.78%)
3. Indonesia — top source of laravel.com doc traffic (7.66%, #1 by that metric); large ASEAN adoption
4. Brazil — ~9% of BuiltWith Laravel sites, strong LATAM presence
5. United Kingdom — historically ~54k sites (older BuiltWith snapshot)
6. Germany — 4.44% of laravel.com traffic
7. Russia — 14,946 current Laravel sites per BuiltWith country breakdown
8. Turkey — 3.8% of laravel.com traffic
9. Philippines / Malaysia — called out by State of Laravel 2026 survey as fastest-growing Southeast Asia markets
10. Japan — flagged by SimilarTech as top-3 adopter alongside US/India

## Sources (primary, fetched 2026-09-13)

- BuiltWith global trend page — https://trends.builtwith.com/ — live Laravel sites worldwide (856,406) and US (362,643)
- BuiltWith country breakdown — https://trends.builtwith.com/framework/country/Russia — Laravel: 14,946
- BuiltWith Papua New Guinea page (lists region links) — https://trends.builtwith.com/framework/Laravel/Papua-New-Guinea — "1,311,683 live sites using Laravel" global figure
- Aguko.com (BuiltWith-derived per-ccTLD stats) — Indonesia .id: 2,407 sites, 12% share; India .in: 5,960 sites, 7.22% share
- GloryWebs "Laravel Usage Statistics 2026" — US 32%, India 17%, Brazil 9%; cites Annual State of Laravel 2026 Survey for Philippines/Malaysia growth
- aundigital.ae — Similarweb traffic to laravel.com: Indonesia 7.66%, India 5.78%, Germany 4.44%, Turkey 3.8%, US 3.79%
- Tempest Blog — Google Trends interest-by-region for "laravel" search term shows highest relative interest in smaller-population countries (Nepal, Myanmar, Cambodia, Armenia) — relative index, not absolute usage, so excluded from ranked list

## Caveats

- BuiltWith counts detected **live websites**, not developer headcount or "monthly active users" — no such metric is publicly tracked.
- Google Trends "interest by region" is a 0–100 relative index normalized per-region population/search-volume, not comparable across countries as an absolute ranking — that's why Nepal/Myanmar top Trends charts despite low absolute site counts.
- Snapshot dates and total-site figures vary a lot between BuiltWith pages fetched same day (362k vs 856k vs 1.3M) — BuiltWith itself is inconsistent across live-count vs historical-count vs a mid-crawl page. Treat the country **ranking order**, not the absolute numbers, as the reliable part.
